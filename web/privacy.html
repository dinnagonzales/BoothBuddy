Booth Buddy — Privacy Policy

Effective August 28, 2026

Booth Buddy is local-first: your shop data stays on your device. We do not run servers, ads, or analytics.

Summary

Booth Buddy is a point-of-sale app for craft-fair and market booths. We do not collect your shop data on our servers — there are none. Sales, inventory, and settings stay on your iPhone or iPad unless you export or share them (for example, a CSV through the iOS share sheet).

We do not sell data, show ads, or use third-party analytics.

Who this app is for

Booth Buddy is listed in the App Store Business category. Staff can log sales at the booth. The Owner sets up items, manages Market Days, and exports data behind a Pass Code. Staff do not see item cost or profit totals.

Information stored on your device

All of the following is stored locally on your device:

DataPurpose
ItemsName, emoji, price, cost, optional photo
Market DaysEvent name and dates
SalesLine items, totals, payment method, cash received, change kept, timestamps
Cancelled salesCancel flag, reason (return / error), optional note — kept for Invoice # history
Sale name & notesOptional labels (e.g. customer name, pickup notes)
Pass Code hashHashed owner gate code (iOS Keychain — not readable)

We never store your Pass Code in plain text.

Information we do not collect

  • No account or login required
  • No transmission of shop data to developer servers
  • No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs
  • No location, contacts, or health data

Photos (optional)

If you add optional item photos, images you choose from the photo library or camera are saved on device only. We do not upload them anywhere.

When data leaves your device

Only when you choose:

  • Export CSV — via Mail, AirDrop, Files, or another app you pick
  • Delete the app or Forgot your code? reset — removes local data

We do not receive copies of exports or shared files.

Children’s privacy

Staff can sell without seeing cost or profit. The owner Pass Code protects owner-only areas. We do not collect children’s information on our servers. Optional sale names or notes an owner enters are stored locally under their control.

Data retention

Data stays on your device until you delete it, reset the app, or uninstall. Booth Buddy v1 has no cloud backup — keep CSV exports you care about.

Security

Shop records live in an on-device database with checks so sale lines stay attached to real sales. App updates that reshape the sales table use a crash-safe migration so a mid-update interruption does not wipe past sales. The Pass Code is stored as a one-way hash in the iOS Keychain. Use a device screen lock and protect your Pass Code.

Changes

We may update this page when the app changes (for example, optional cloud sync in a future Pro tier). The effective date at the top will change when we do.
