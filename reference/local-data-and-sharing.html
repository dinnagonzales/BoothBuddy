Glossary

Sandbox — Private folder iOS/Android gives your app; other apps cannot read it.

Share sheet — OS popup listing Mail, Messages, AirDrop, Save to Files.

Parental gate — Simple challenge (math problem, hold button) before leaving kid-safe flows.
